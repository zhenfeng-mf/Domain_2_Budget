## 2. Actors

| Actor | What they want | What they can do | What they must not be able to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| メンバー | Get an expense approved without wasting a cycle | Draft, edit/delete own draft, submit, view own requests and states | Set budgets, approve, reject, touch another member's records |
| 承認者 | Judge a request quickly and never overspend the budget | Set monthly budgets per category, approve, reject, view all requests and summaries | (decide: approve their own expense — see Q-06) |

## 3. Core value loop

> An approver can open one screen, see the true remaining budget, and approve or reject in seconds — confident that the team can never overspend the budget, even by accident.
> 

**Value moment:** the instant the approval succeeds and the new remaining budget is restated.

## 6. Ambiguities — my assumption and why

The spec does not answer these. Recording the decision *and the tradeoff* is worth more than silently picking one.

| # | Ambiguity | My assumption | Why / tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-01 | Can a member edit a 申請済み expense? | Yes | It’s easy for one to make mistakes when entering #s. The system should tolerate this kind of common mistakes. |
| A-02 | Is 却下 terminal, or editable and resubmittable? | It’s tereminal. | To keep the log of requests. Members need to submit a new one if they get rejected |
| A-03 | Can an expense be submitted when no budget exists for its month + category? | No | The budget is fixed and every approved request cannot be withdraw. We should not allow making a request that is impossible to approve |
| A-04 | Can an approver approve their own expense? | No | This is what all corps do.  |
| A-05 | One approver globally, or one per team? | One approver can be in charge for multiple teams. A team should have one approver(FK), and multiple members(not sure if a member should only stay in one team) | This suits the real scenario. |
| A-06 | What happens when a draft's 利用日 moves to a different month? | draft: Allow; Other status: immutable | folds the real risk into the state guard you're already building for I-05 — one mechanism, two invariants protected. |
| A-07 | What is the UX when lowering a budget below approved spend? | Not allowed. Budget adjustment only for the coming months not current or past. | otherwise problems coming. |
